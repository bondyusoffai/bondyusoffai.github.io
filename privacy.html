Privacy policy

Bond GWS CLI. Last updated 13 October 2026.

Who uses this app

Bond GWS CLI is a private command-line tool used by one person, its owner, with their own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail, Tasks, Drive, Docs and Sheets. It uses this access only to carry out commands the owner runs.

Where the data goes

  • Data is read and used on the owner's own computer.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on that computer.
  • The app has no server and no database of its own.
  • The owner may use their own data with their own tools, including AI assistants, for their own work.
  • No data is sold or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
